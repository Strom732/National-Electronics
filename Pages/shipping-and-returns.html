Returns, Refunds & Warranty Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026
Important: All products are tested and inspected before shipment to ensure they are functioning properly at the time of dispatch.

Product Testing

At National Electronics, every product is carefully inspected and tested (where applicable) before being packed and shipped. This process helps ensure that customers receive products in working condition.

Warranty Policy

No Warranty Provided: National Electronics does not provide any warranty or guarantee unless specifically mentioned in the product description.

Any manufacturer warranty that may exist is governed solely by the manufacturer's terms and conditions. Customers should contact the manufacturer directly regarding warranty-related matters.

No Returns Policy

All Sales Are Final. Due to pre-shipment testing and inspection, we generally do not accept returns once an order has been delivered.

Returns will not be accepted for:

  • Change of mind
  • Incorrect purchase decisions
  • Compatibility issues
  • Customer preferences
  • Used or opened products
  • Products damaged after delivery due to misuse

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive an incorrect item or a product that was visibly damaged during transit, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

Please provide:

  • Your order number
  • Photos of the package
  • Photos of the product received

Claims submitted after 24 hours of delivery may not be eligible for review.

Refund Policy

Refunds are only considered when:

  • An order cannot be fulfilled by National Electronics.
  • An incorrect item was shipped and verified by our team.
  • An issue is determined to have occurred on our side.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method.
